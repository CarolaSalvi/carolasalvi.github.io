Optional website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-RVZL6YHRDQ) to understand aggregate website use, including page visits and approximate geographic information about visitors.

When enabled, Google Analytics may process information about pages viewed, device and browser characteristics, interactions, approximate location, and online identifiers. Google Analytics is provided by Google.

Analytics is optional and is activated only after you select "Accept" in the cookie banner. The legal basis for optional analytics cookies and related tracking is your consent. You may refuse analytics without losing access to the website.

Your cookie choice

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key carola_analytics_consent. This allows the website to remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics.

To change your preference, select Cookie settings on the main website. You can also clear your browser's site data.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. As with most website hosting services, technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request data, may be processed to deliver the website and maintain security.

How long is information kept?

Your consent choice remains in your browser's local storage until you change it or clear your site data. Google Analytics data retention depends on the settings configured for the analytics property.
